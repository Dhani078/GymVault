## 🇮🇩 Bahasa Indonesia (ID) — Yang Baru di Versi 2.5.0

• Mode Latihan Offline di Gym Bawah Tanah: Sekarang Anda dapat mencatat seluruh set, beban, dan repetisi tanpa koneksi internet (PWA offline-first). Data otomatis tersinkronisasi saat sinyal kembali.  
• Pencatatan Suara Bebas Tangan (Voice Logger): Catat repetisi sambil memegang dumbbell berat hanya dengan berbicara ("Coach, catat 80 kilo 8 repetisi").  
• Kalkulator Olympic Barbell & Micro-Plates: Simulasi akurat penataan piringan beban Olimpiade hingga piringan presisi 1.25kg per sisi.  
• Peningkatan Ketahanan & Pemulihan Sesi: Sistem perlindungan crash baru menjaga seluruh catatan latihan Anda tetap aman bahkan jika terjadi gangguan tak terduga.  
• Pembayaran QRIS Otomatis & Cepat: Pembayaran langganan GymVault Pro kini diproses secara instan melalui QRIS berlisensi resmi dengan aktivasi instan 3 detik.  
• Dukungan Aksesibilitas: Antarmuka AMOLED ramah pembaca layar (screen reader) dan tombol aksi yang lebih mudah disentuh saat tangan berkeringat.

## 🇬🇧 English (EN) — What's New in Version 2.5.0

• Basement Gym Offline Mode: Log heavy sets, weights, and reps seamlessly even with zero cell reception. Offline sync queue auto-uploads your workouts as soon as you reconnect.  
• Hands-Free Voice Workout Logger: Speak commands naturally while holding heavy dumbbells to log sets, adjust reps, and trigger rest countdown timers.  
• Olympic Barbell & Micro-Plate Math: Instant barbell loading breakdowns supporting official Olympic bumper plates down to 1.25kg micro-plates.  
• Rock-Solid Session Recovery: Root-level crash guard ensures your workout logs and biometric metrics are preserved under all conditions.  
• Instant QRIS Pro Subscriptions: Lightning-fast automated QRIS checkout with instant 3-second account provisioning.  
• Enhanced Mobile Accessibility: Screen reader optimized controls and high-contrast AMOLED visuals designed for high-intensity gym sessions.
